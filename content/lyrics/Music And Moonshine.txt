Grandma told me how her folks got free
With nothing but their hands
They worked a patch in Tennessee
Till hard times hit the land

Grandpa turned the corn into shine
In the barn, he sold it by the jar
Grandma tuned her old guitar
She took a sip, smiled at him, and said

We got the fire, we got all night
Come on, boys, let the music come alive
Keep it rolling till the morning light

Music and moonshine!

Now the whole crowd sings along
We worked all day, now we’re dancing all night long
Raise a glass to the ones who kept holding on
